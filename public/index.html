FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS

Common Queries About Admissions & Campus

Candidates can choose Sha's Arts and Science College during the Tamil Nadu collegiate admissions single-window counseling or apply directly through our official admissions portal. Our admission desk is open 6 days a week to guide students through the choice-filling procedure.

Hostel accommodation is optional but highly recommended for students traveling from outside Kanchipuram and Chennai. We provide furnished rooms (2-sharing and 3-sharing), solar water heating, high-speed Wi-Fi, and nutritious vegetarian/non-vegetarian dining.

SASC offers up to 75% tuition fee waiver for students scoring 95%+ in their 12th board exams. Additionally, Government First Graduate scholarships, Post-Matric SC/ST grants, and sports/cultural excellence scholarships are actively facilitated.

Candidates who have passed Higher Secondary (10+2) examinations with required minimum aggregate marks (Science stream for B.Sc CS/IT, Commerce stream for B.Com/BBA, or any recognized stream for B.A./BCA) are eligible to apply.
